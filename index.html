Exercise 1: Fix the visit details toggle

  1. Make it a real button.
  2. Add aria-expanded and aria-controls.
  3. Keep aria-expanded in sync in JavaScript.
  4. Check the Accessibility pane. Paste your button and JS in chat.

    Exercise 2: Fix the appointment form

    1. Give every input a visible, tied label.
    2. Tie the hint to the email field.
    3. Group the radios with their question.
    4. Click each label: does focus move? Paste the email field’s computed name and description in chat.

      1. Developers: “File saved” polite, “Upload failed” urgent.

      4. Everyone: focus never moves.
